How it works

Three straightforward steps to benchmark your technical profile and prioritize what to learn.

1

Upload your resume

Provide your resume as a PDF or Word document. Cairn extracts your programming skills, experience timeline, and project claims.

2

Add the job description

Paste the job description text or upload a job document. Cairn extracts required competencies and identifies the hiring company.

3

Read your results and plan

Review your match score, missing skills breakdown, ATS risk findings, GitHub proof signals, and a customized week-by-week study roadmap.

What you get

Actionable diagnostics built specifically for software engineering and data roles.

Match score and skill gaps

A clear match percentage comparing your resume against the job description, showing exactly which required skills are confirmed and which are missing.

Readiness for a company

A weighted competency score across 12 skill areas (Coding, System Design, SQL, Cloud) benchmarked against vetted hiring bars at 113 engineering companies.

ATS check for hidden text

GitHub check of your projects

Study roadmap

A deterministic, week-by-week study plan with concrete practice exercises and proof-project ideas that produce visible code evidence on GitHub.

Clear commitment: Your files are used only to produce this analysis. Cairn does not read LinkedIn, and results are estimates, not hiring decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers about our analysis methodology and security standards.

How is the job match score calculated?

Cairn extracts the technical requirements directly from the provided job description and scans your resume for verifiable evidence of each requirement. Confirmed skills increase the match score, while unmentioned skills appear in your missing skills list.

Does Cairn scrape or store my LinkedIn data?

No. Cairn never scrapes or stores private LinkedIn profiles. If your resume contains a LinkedIn link, we detect it for your review, but never execute network requests against LinkedIn.

What does the GitHub check look for?

We query public GitHub REST data to examine repositories matching your resume claims. We evaluate commit frequency over time, test coverage, and documentation hygiene. All outputs are clearly labeled as signals, not proof.

Can I customize my study plan?

Yes. On the analysis page, you can choose how many hours per week you have available (from 2 to 30 hours) and your target preparation timeline (up to 24 weeks) to recalculate an achievable schedule.
