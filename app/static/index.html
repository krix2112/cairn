FOUNDATIONS & BOUNDARIES

Method & Honest Limits

Cairn is an engineering instrument for skill calibration, not an opaque screening algorithm. Here is our exact operational contract.

What Cairn Measures

  • 12 Competency Baselines Every candidate is calibrated across 12 discrete engineering vectors rather than collapsed into an unhelpful aggregate score.
  • 113 Corporate Hiring Benchmarks Target slot dimensions reflect verified empirical requirements from tier-1 engineering organizations.
  • Evidence-Anchored Scoring Mass is awarded only when skills are validated by project implementations, production internships, or academic accomplishments.
  • Asymmetric Gravity Surplus in one skill does not excuse missing foundations. A cairn with no bedrock cannot support a heavier summit.

What Cairn Does Not Do

  • Not an Automated Hiring Gate Cairn is a diagnostic roadmap for engineers to understand their standing, never an automated rejection filter for hiring teams.
  • Estimated from Text Evidence Skill levels reflect the fidelity of written resumes and project descriptions. Omitted achievements cannot be credited by the parser.
  • Static Snapshot of Trajectory The score measures your current footing, not your capacity to learn or velocity in the first ninety days on the job.
  • Sample Data Flagged Demonstration candidates and synthetic test records are explicitly tagged as sample data across the entire interface.
